About Basket Money King

Arcade skill game. No real money, no betting. All money in the game is virtual and has no cash value.

What is it?

Basket Money King is a free arcade game you play in your web browser. Coins and notes fall from the sky, and you catch them with a net. Avoid the bombs, build combos, grab boosts and reach the target before time runs out.

How to play

  • Hand mode: point your phone's camera at your fist. The net follows your hand.
  • Touch mode: drag your finger on the screen to move the net.
  • Each level is a little faster and harder. You have 5 lives, and lost lives refill over time.

Your camera stays private

In hand mode, the camera picture is processed only on your own device to find your hand. It is never recorded, saved or sent to anyone.
