Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information Basket Money King ("the game", "we") collects and how it is used. The game is owned and published by MBITA MBALA JOSEPH JUNIOR, Cameroon.

1. Camera

The current version of the game does not use your camera. If a camera-controlled mode is offered in the future, it will only start after you choose it and allow it in your browser, the camera picture will be processed only on your device, and camera images will never be recorded, stored or uploaded.

2. Information saved on your device

The game saves your level, best scores, remaining lives and settings in your browser's local storage so you can continue later. This information stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Accounts and leaderboards

When you open the game, an anonymous guest account is created automatically so your level and scores can be saved. For this account we store a random account ID, your leaderboard name, your highest level and your level results (level, money caught, win or loss, time). If you later sign in with email, we also store your email address. This is used only to run your account and the leaderboards. Leaderboard names and scores are visible to other players.

4. Advertising

The game may show ads provided by third parties such as Google. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads and measure them, including ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

5. Payments

If optional in-game purchases are offered, payments are handled by a payment provider. We do not see or store your card or mobile money details. We only receive confirmation that a payment was made.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with service providers needed to run the game (hosting, database, ads, payments), and when required by law.

7. Children

The game is family-friendly, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices

You can play without creating an account (a guest account is made automatically), clear the game's saved data in your browser, and ask us to delete any account data by contacting us.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
